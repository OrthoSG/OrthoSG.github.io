EXAMPLE PROMPTS

Find a starting point.

SprintA focused ten minutes

I am a medical student revising for orthopaedic finals. I have 10 minutes. Run a sprint on hip fractures: ask three questions, one at a time. Wait for my answer, explain my biggest gap and finish with a short recap. Flag uncertainty and do not invent references.

SBAExplain your choice

Give me five formative orthopaedic single best answer questions, one at a time. Hide the answer until I commit and explain my reasoning. Then explain each option and revisit my weakest topic with a new question.

OSCERehearse your structure

Run a mock knee examination OSCE. Give me only the candidate task first. Wait until I say “done” before giving formative feedback on my sequence and explanation. Let me repeat the weakest section. Do not infer hands-on competence from my description.

ImagingPractise a systematic read

Give me a fictional, text-described wrist X-ray case for finals revision. Guide me through a systematic read, asking one question at a time. Wait for my interpretation before explaining. Keep the case educational and clearly label it as fictional.

Voice vivaSay your reasoning aloud

Give me a five-minute orthopaedic viva in coach mode. Ask one short question at a time. Please wait until I say “done”. Then correct my biggest gap and test it in a different fictional case. Keep your responses brief.

Revision planningPlan what to revisit

Help me plan seven days of orthopaedic revision with 20 minutes a day. First ask about my weak areas and learning objectives. Mix retrieval, cases and review. At the end of each session, give me a short revision summary to copy into my next chat.
