Evidence and safety

Formative, source-conscious, and clear about its limits.

OrthoSG is an educational revision tool. Clinical content is curated and progressively source-verified against authoritative references. Where a recommendation depends on local institutional policy, this is explicitly flagged for local verification rather than presented as universal.

OrthoSG does not certify competence and does not replace clinical supervision, institutional protocols or professional judgement.
